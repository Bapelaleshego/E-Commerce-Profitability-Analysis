<h2 align="left">E-Commerce Profitability & Marketing Spend Analysis for BrightCart</h2>

###

<h4 align="left">Executive Summary</h4>

###

<p align="left">I used Excel to analyse the profitability and marketing spend of a direct-to-consumer e-commerce brand called BrightCart. During my analysis I identified the top and bottom performing product categories and sales channel. I also identified marketing platforms that are delivering the best return on ad spend. I recommended that the Sales and Marketing departments implements a few adjustments to increase profit margins:<br><br>1. Review pricing on certain products<br>2. Renegotiate fee on sales channels with low ROI<br>3. Improve product descriptions and size guides<br>4. Recommended 20% budget reductions</p>

###

<h4 align="left">Business Problem</h4>

###

<p align="left">BrightCart is an online retailer selling products across 8 categories and 4 sales channels. The company made $1M+ in gross revenue over the past two years however the net margins have been shrinking and profitability is uneven.</p>

###

<h4 align="left">Methodology</h4>

###

<p align="left">1. Excel to extract, clean, transform the data and perform data analysis<br>2. PowerPoint to compile a report with recommendations</p>

###

<h4 align="left">Skills</h4>

###

<p align="left">1. Excel: Power Query, Data Modelling, ETL, Dax and Data Visualization<br>2. PowerPoint: Report writing</p>

###

<h4 align="left">Results & Recommendation</h4>

###

<p align="left">I compiled a report summarising the business" performance over the past two years. The key takeaways and recommendations from the overall analysis include:<br><br>-  Some products suffer the most return, discount rates and under-pricing which can be resolved by improving the product info, size guides and improving pricing strategy.<br>- TikTok and Instagram must be prioritized since they deliver the highest ROI.<br>- Less investment should be implemented on Marketplace and Social Commerce, they have the lowest order quantity and high return rates.<br><br>I further suggested a 20% budget reduction on platforms depending on months they under performs.</p>
